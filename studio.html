Studio

We create spaces that feel inevitable.

LUMEN was founded in 2019 by a small group of architects and interior designers who were tired of interiors that photographed well and lived badly. We work on residential, hospitality, and small commercial projects across Mumbai, Pune, and the coastal towns nearby.

Philosophy

We think of interior architecture as an act of editing more than decorating. Most rooms don't need more — they need the right few things, placed with enough conviction that nothing else is required.

That means slower material choices, fewer statement pieces, and a willingness to leave a wall bare if a bare wall is what the room actually needs. Our clients tend to be people who've lived in over-styled spaces before and are ready for something quieter.

It also means we stay involved past the design phase. Furniture layouts, lighting temperature, even where a family keeps its shoes — the small decisions are usually the ones that determine whether a beautiful plan becomes a beautiful home.
