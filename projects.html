Selected Work

Four rooms, one way of looking at light.

A small, deliberately limited body of residential and commercial work across Mumbai, Alibaug, and Pune — each project developed around the same three questions: how light enters, what the materials will look like in ten years, and what the room is actually for.

Residence No. 01
