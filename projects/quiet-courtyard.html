Residential

Quiet Courtyard

Pune 2025

One tree, four rooms

A courtyard instead of a corridor.

The site was narrow and hemmed in on three sides, which usually forces a house into a dark central corridor. Instead, we pulled every room around a small open courtyard, so each one borrows light and air from the same source rather than from a shared hallway.

Location

Pune

Year

2025

Type

Residential

Area

2,150 sq ft

Status

Completed

Design Approach

01

Light

Every room takes its natural light from the courtyard rather than an exterior wall, since the site's three shared boundaries made exterior windows impractical.

02

Material

Local stone flooring runs continuously from the courtyard into the ground floor, blurring where outside ends and inside begins.

03

Proportion

Rooms are kept small and close to the courtyard edge rather than deep and enclosed, so no space is ever more than a few steps from daylight.

04

Function

The courtyard itself was designed to work as a working space in the cooler months, not just a view — it has power and shade for exactly that reason.
