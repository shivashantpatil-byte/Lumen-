Residential

Coastal House

Alibaug 2025

Built for salt air, not against it

A house that expects to be left open.

Most coastal homes fight the climate with sealed glass and air conditioning. Coastal House does the opposite — deep overhangs, cross-ventilation, and finishes chosen specifically because they're meant to weather rather than be protected from it.

Location

Alibaug

Year

2025

Type

Residential

Area

2,800 sq ft

Status

Completed

Design Approach

01

Light

Deep roof overhangs cut the harsh midday glare while still letting low morning and evening light reach into every main room.

02

Material

Lime plaster, teak, and local stone were chosen because they're expected to weather visibly — a patina, not a maintenance problem.

03

Proportion

Rooms are wider and shorter than usual, favoring open sightlines to the water over the tall, narrow volumes used at Residence No. 01.

04

Function

Every ground-floor room has a direct outdoor connection, since the house is only ever occupied on weekends and needs to feel used immediately on arrival.
