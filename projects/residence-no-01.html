Residential

Residence No. 01

Mumbai 2026

A study in quiet proportion

Built around one wall of light.

Residence No. 01 began with a simple complaint: the original plan lit every room evenly and none of them well. We removed a load-bearing partition, ran the main living spaces along a single east-facing glazed wall, and let the rest of the plan organize itself around that light.

Location

Mumbai

Year

2026

Type

Residential

Area

3,400 sq ft

Status

Completed

Design Approach

01

Light

The east wall was fixed before anything else — every subsequent decision, including which rooms got doors, followed from where the morning sun would land.

02

Material

Travertine and white oak were chosen to warm up rather than dull with age, since this is a family home, not a showroom.

03

Proportion

Ceiling heights were raised only in the living wing; the private rooms stay lower and more enclosed by comparison, so the house reads as a sequence rather than one uniform volume.

04

Function

A mudroom and a second, smaller kitchen were added off the entry specifically so the main kitchen could stay closed off during large family gatherings.
